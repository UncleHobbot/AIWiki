## Summary

Opening lecture of Yandex Education's Agents Week 2026 intensive, covering the evolution of human-LLM interaction, what LLMs are (weights, next-token prediction), how they train, what they do well, and building a basic agent in practice. Speaker: Alena Zaitseva, head of AI services at Yandex Lavka.

## Key Ideas
- **Agent evolution:** From simple LLM calls to autonomous agents — the evolution of human-LLM interaction patterns
- **LLM fundamentals:** Weights, next-token prediction, training mechanics — what's under the hood of GPT, Claude, etc.
- **Practical agent building:** Hands-on construction of a simple agent during the lecture
- **Yandex production context:** Alena Zaitseva leads AI service development at Yandex Lavka (grocery delivery), building both customer-facing and internal AI agents plus the platform for them
- **Part of a full intensive course:** Agents Week 2026 is a multi-day Yandex Education program on AI agent development

## Details

This is the opening lecture of Yandex Education's Agents Week 2026 — a structured intensive course on building AI agents. The speaker, Alena Zaitseva, heads AI services at Yandex Lavka, one of Russia's largest grocery delivery platforms, where she oversees both customer-facing and internal AI agents plus the underlying platform.

The lecture covers foundational concepts: what LLMs are at a technical level (weights, next-token prediction), how they're trained, where they excel, and culminates in a hands-on session building a basic agent. This is notable as a Tier 2 educational resource from a major tech company's internal training program.

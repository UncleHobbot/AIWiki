## Summary
OpenAI previewed three new models — **GPT-5.6 Sol**, **Terra**, and **Luna** — to approved partners with a deliberately restricted rollout and stronger safeguards tuned for cyber-defense use cases. The limited-access framing (rather than general release) marks OpenAI's continued caution around frontier cyber capability.

## Key Ideas
- **Three preview models:** GPT-5.6 Sol (the headline), plus Terra and Luna — previewed to approved partners only.
- **Restricted access by design:** not a general release; part of OpenAI's pattern of staging frontier cyber-capable models to vetted users.
- **Stronger cyber safeguards:** tuned for cyber-defense use cases (the defensive framing matches OpenAI's recent policy direction).
- Tier 2 (reputable, The Hacker News reporting on OpenAI preview).
- Follows the broader 2026 frontier-release cadence (Claude Fable 5, DeepSeek V4, GLM-5.2, Kimi K2.7 Code).

## Details
Limited-access previews are how OpenAI is handling models with advanced cyber capabilities — vetted partners first, general availability later. Sol is the flagship name in this batch; Terra and Luna's positioning (coding vs reasoning vs multimodal) isn't fully detailed in the source. Worth tracking for the public release and benchmark numbers.
